Từ 1 / 20
01 Nhấn vào thẻ để xem nghĩa

Tráng miệng — Luyện tập

4 bài luyện, mỗi bài đều có nút "Xem hóa đơn" để hiện đáp án.

Chọn định nghĩa phù hợp cho mỗi từ, sau đó xem hóa đơn để kiểm tra.

Hóa đơn — Luyện nói theo cặp

8 đoạn hội thoại mẫu về chủ đề nhà hàng. Đọc theo cặp A–B; các từ vựng in đậm là từ đã học hôm nay.

1Making a reservation
A
Should I make a reservation for our client lunch?
B
Yes. Please ask for a table in the dining area.
A
Sure. Should I also order some appetizers?
B
Yes, and we can choose the main course when they arrive.
2Dietary restrictions
A
I made a reservation for twelve o'clock.
B
Great. Did you ask about Mr. Brown's dietary restrictions?
A
Yes. He cannot eat seafood.
B
Okay. Let's ask the server about the available dishes.
3Ordering food
A
What would you like for your entrée?
B
I'd like the chicken, please.
A
Would you like a beverage or a dessert?
B
Yes, I'd like some coffee and a small dessert.
4Paying the bill
A
Excuse me, could we have the receipt, please?
B
Sure. Is the service charge included?
A
Yes, it is.
B
Great. Please put the bill on the company account.
5A complimentary item
A
Excuse me, is this dessert complimentary?
B
Yes. It comes with the business lunch.
A
That's great. Could we also order two beverages?
B
Of course. I'll ask the server.
6Busy restaurant
A
Do we have a reservation for 1 p.m.?
B
Yes, but the restaurant is very busy today.
A
Is there a waitlist?
B
Yes, but our table should be ready in about ten minutes.
7Takeout
A
I'm not very hungry. Can I order takeout?
B
Sure. What would you like?
A
I'd like the chicken entrée and a beverage.
B
Okay. I'll place the order for you.
8More natural / business-like
A
Have you made a reservation for our client?
B
Yes. I booked a table in the quiet dining area.
A
Perfect. Did you tell the restaurant about his dietary restrictions?
B
Yes. They said they could prepare a special meal for him.
